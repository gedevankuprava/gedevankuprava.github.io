What I do

Take a locked global master and rebuild it as a complete local format set — every ratio, every duration, every channel size, in an alphabet the original was never drawn for, without breaking the brand. That is what the Samsung and Nestlé work on this site is. The Georgian accounts are the opposite job: written from nothing, then held together across a whole season. The alphabet happens to be Georgian; the discipline is the same in any market.
