Over the past seven years, I have developed integrated campaigns at two Tbilisi-based agencies, including dentsu's affiliate in Georgia. Each case study clearly outlines the scope of my contribution: whether developed from concept, adapted for the Georgian market, or delivered through a combination of original creation and localisation.
