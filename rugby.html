Georgian Rugby Union

A Unified Visual Identity for a Full Rugby Season

I developed a cohesive visual identity and Georgian typography system for the Georgian Rugby Union, applied consistently across match-day communications, ticketing campaigns, sponsor assets and league content, from the domestic season to the EPCR Challenge Cup.

One key visual, adapted across every placement

The key visual was individually adapted for each format, with refined Georgian typography, carefully reframed player imagery and restructured sponsor lock-ups to maintain clarity, balance and consistency across every placement.

Shown at true relative scale.

Match key visuals: one layout per fixture

Role
Created: the visual language and the layout system. The Georgian type, the sponsor lock-ups and the player photography treatment, rebuilt as a separate layout for every placement the season ran in.
Scale
One system, on the union's own social channels and on national television, held for a full season. It carried a routine weekly fixture and an EPCR Challenge Cup night at the same standard, from first whistle to last.
Authorship
Created for the Georgian Rugby Union: the visual language and every layout, from zero, for a full season.
