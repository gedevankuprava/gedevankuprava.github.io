Coca-Cola Georgia

A Rewards App, Seasonal Campaigns and Integrated Brand Communications

For Coca-Cola Georgia, I developed and adapted creative assets across its rewards platform, mini-games, seasonal campaigns, packaging, calendars and digital media.

Rewards app: referral, mini game, prize draw

Seasonal campaigns, square and portrait

Printed work: labels, calendars, banners

Film and motion

Role
My role combined the localisation of global campaign materials with the creation of original assets, ensuring consistency across every touchpoint while adhering to Coca-Cola's global brand guidelines.
Scale
Three years on one account that runs two clocks at once. The rewards app and its mini-game have to keep working release after release. The seasonal print, the labels and the calendars are fixed the moment they go to press.
Authorship
Half created for the account outright, half adapted from Coca-Cola's own campaign material, inside the same global brand book throughout.
Years
2023–2025
