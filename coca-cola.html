Coca-Cola Georgia

The app, the festival and the printed label

Half of this account is Coca-Cola's own material refitted to Georgian channels; half was made here. It runs from the rewards app and its mini-game through a festival identity to labels, calendars and printed banner material.

Role
Half adapted from Coca-Cola's own material, half created outright. That is my own statement of the split, and it is not mapped piece by piece.
Scale
Rewards-app screens and a mini-game, a festival identity in three colourways, seasonal and promotional artwork, printed calendars, labels and banner material.
