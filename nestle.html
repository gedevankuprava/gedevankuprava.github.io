Film and motion

Role
Re-cutting Nestlé's finished global films into whatever duration and ratio each Georgian placement needs, and re-setting the Georgian type across the still key visuals.
Scale
Six brands on one account, seven years running. Every campaign reaches Georgia already finished, as a film or a key visual with nothing behind it, and every Georgian duration, ratio and crop still has to be cut out of that.
Authorship
Adapted for the local market: six Nestlé brands' global films and key visuals, re-cut and re-set for every Georgian placement.
