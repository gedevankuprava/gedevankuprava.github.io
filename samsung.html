Samsung

One campaign, every format Georgia runs

Samsung's global campaigns arrive as a master artwork and a set of rules. Getting them on air in Georgia means rebuilding each one at the size, ratio and script the local channel actually uses — and keeping the brand intact while the type changes alphabet. That meant a full local set per campaign: key visuals, retail and out of home, film cutdowns — not a translated copy of the global one.

Role
Adaptation for the local market: master artwork in, a complete local format set out. Georgian headlines re-set, line breaks rebuilt, optical size matched to the Latin brand lock-ups they sit beside.
Scale
One key visual, rebuilt rather than resized, into a full local set across five ratios — each with its own crop, type scale and safe area.

Women's Day — one campaign, every placement 18

One campaign, eighteen finished files — 1600×1000, 1200×1500, 900×1600, 1080×1080, and eleven single-product banners at 320×480 and 341×652. Every caption is the real delivered size. Each file is a separate build: its own crop, its own type size, its own line breaks in Georgian, its own safe area, and in most of them a different product in the frame. No global master of this campaign exists in the archive I hold, so master and local cannot honestly be shown side by side — this is the output half of the claim, and it is the half I can prove.
