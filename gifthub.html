Gifthub

A product-card system for a Georgian gift marketplace

Gifthub is a Georgian gift marketplace selling handmade goods: dolls, jewellery, beard care, textiles. I created the one system that carries its full product range and every campaign built on top of it.

Product cards and campaign artwork

Film and motion

Role
Created: the product-card layout and the campaign artwork.
Scale
One year, and a product catalogue whose categories have nothing in common. Different materials, different shapes. The same card layout gave all of it one identity, carried across social media and television both.
Authorship
Created for Gifthub. The layout and every piece of campaign artwork built on it.
